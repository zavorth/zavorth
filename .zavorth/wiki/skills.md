## Purpose

Capture stable facts about Zavorth-native skills and their evolution loop.

## Current Facts

- Skills are governed capabilities with metadata, risk profile, allowed tools,
  and approval boundaries.
- The curator can preview quality, duplication, umbrella merges, and evolution
  proposals.
- Applying skill changes requires approval.

## Decisions

- Skills should remain Zavorth-native in structure, naming, metadata, and
  receipts.
- High-risk operations stay missions or governed tools, not silent skills.
