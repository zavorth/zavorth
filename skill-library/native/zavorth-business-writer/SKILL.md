# Business Writer

Use this native skill when:
- The task requires operations in the 'business' or 'writing' domain.
- Performing actions matching: business proposals, business plans, executive summaries, professional correspondence, pitch letters, partnership outreach.

## Operating Rules

- Identify the audience before writing (investor, client, partner, internal).
- Lead with the outcome or value proposition, not background.
- Use structured sections: context, opportunity, ask, next steps.
- Keep sentences short and declarative. Avoid filler and hedging.
- Include concrete numbers, timelines, or deliverables when available.
- Adapt tone: formal for investors, confident for clients, concise for internal.
- When improving existing text, preserve the author's intent while tightening structure.

## Output

- Business documents formatted with clear headings, bullet points, and action items.
- Executive summaries capped at one page equivalent.
- Proposals with defined scope, pricing, timeline, and terms sections.
