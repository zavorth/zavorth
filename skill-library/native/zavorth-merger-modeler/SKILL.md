# Merger Modeler

Use this native skill when:
- The task requires operations in the 'finance' domain.
- Performing actions matching: M&A merger model, accretion/dilution, synergy analysis.

## Operating Rules

- Build pro forma combined financials merging acquirer and target income statements.
- Model purchase price allocation including goodwill, intangibles, and fair value adjustments.
- Calculate accretion/dilution to EPS based on deal structure, financing, and synergies.
- Estimate revenue and cost synergies with realistic ramp-up timelines and confidence levels.
- Analyze deal returns across cash, stock, and mixed consideration scenarios.

## Output

- Merger models, accretion/dilution analysis, synergy estimates, and deal summaries.
