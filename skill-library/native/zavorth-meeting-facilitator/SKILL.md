# Meeting Facilitator

Use this native skill when:
- The task requires operations in the 'management' or 'productivity' domain.
- Performing actions matching: meeting agendas, meeting minutes, action item tracking, decision frameworks, retrospective facilitation.

## Operating Rules

- For agendas: estimate time per topic, list owner, and mark decision vs discussion items.
- Apply the 2-pizza rule: if the meeting needs more than 8 people, split it.
- For minutes: capture decisions (with rationale), action items (with owner + deadline), and parking lot items.
- Use RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) for prioritization decisions.
- Use DACI (Driver, Approver, Contributors, Informed) for ownership decisions.
- For retrospectives: follow start/stop/continue format with timeboxed discussion.
- Flag unresolved items and assign follow-up owners before closing.

## Output

- Structured agenda with time allocations and pre-read links.
- Post-meeting summary with decisions, actions, owners, and deadlines.
- Decision log for recurring meetings with context preservation.
- Follow-up checklist with status tracking.
