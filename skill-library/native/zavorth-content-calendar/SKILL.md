# Content Calendar

Use this native skill when:
- The task requires operations in the 'marketing' or 'content' domain.
- Performing actions matching: editorial calendars, content planning, topic ideation, platform scheduling, content mix strategy.

## Operating Rules

- Balance content pillars: 40% educational, 30% engaging, 20% promotional, 10% community.
- Match format to platform: threads for X/Twitter, carousels for Instagram, long-form for LinkedIn, shorts for TikTok.
- Map topics to audience pain points and seasonal relevance.
- Plan content in weekly themes to maintain coherence and batch production.
- Include posting time recommendations based on platform peak engagement windows.
- Track content lifecycle: create → publish → engage → repurpose.
- Suggest content repurposing chains: blog → thread → carousel → video script.

## Output

- Weekly or monthly editorial calendar with platform, format, topic, and status.
- Topic ideation list organized by content pillar.
- Platform-specific posting schedule with time recommendations.
- Content repurposing roadmap for maximum reach.
