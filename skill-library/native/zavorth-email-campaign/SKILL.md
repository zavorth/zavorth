# Email Campaign

Use this native skill when:
- The task requires operations in the 'marketing' domain.
- Performing actions matching: Email campaign management, template design, analytics tracking.

## Operating Rules

- Include unsubscribe links in all marketing emails for compliance.
- Segment audiences based on engagement and preference data.
- A/B test subject lines and content before full campaign launch.
- Monitor bounce rates and sender reputation metrics.
- Validate email templates across multiple clients before sending.

## Output

- Campaign reports, A/B test results, delivery metrics, and engagement analytics.
