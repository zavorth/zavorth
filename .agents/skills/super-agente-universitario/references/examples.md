# Examples

## Explicit invocation

`Use $super-agente-universitario to read this article, explain the key ideas, and then create exam-style questions.`

## Book or chapter

`Use $super-agente-universitario to analyze this chapter, extract the main thesis, and explain it at an intermediate software engineering level.`

## Video summary

`Use $super-agente-universitario on this lecture summary and turn it into a structured study guide plus five oral questions.`

## Assignment review

`Use $super-agente-universitario to review my assignment answer, point out conceptual mistakes, and tell me how to improve it.`

## Mock exam

`Use $super-agente-universitario to create a 12-question mock exam on operating systems with mixed difficulty and a commented answer key.`

## Debate

`Use $super-agente-universitario to debate whether AI tools improve or weaken student learning, then give me a balanced synthesis.`

## Reflective conversation

`Use $super-agente-universitario to help me think through my stress about college, study performance, and future plans.`

## One-question oral drill

`Use $super-agente-universitario to run an oral exam on databases. Ask one question at a time and only reveal the model answer after I reply.`
