# Brand Guide - Zavorth

## Mascot: The Fox

The fox is the visual mascot of Zavorth. It represents watchfulness, quick thinking, and quiet competence.

### Visual identity
- **Species:** Fox
- **Color palette:** Zavorth green (#00e88f) + dark backgrounds (#060809)
- **Personality:** Calm, exact, prepared. Never flashy. Always ready.
- **Usage:** The fox appears in the logo, onboarding screens, and product surfaces. The mascot is NOT a separate agent — Zavorth is the agent, the fox is the visual face.

### Do's and Don'ts
- **Do:** Use the mascot to make the product feel approachable and memorable
- **Do:** Keep the mascot's expression calm and competent
- **Don't:** Make the mascot talk or have a separate personality from Zavorth
- **Don't:** Use the mascot in security-critical contexts (approvals, receipts)

## Brand voice

### Tone
- **Clear:** Say what you mean without ambiguity
- **Direct:** Skip the ceremony and filler
- **Competent:** Show you know what you're doing
- **Warm:** Be human without being fake

### Taglines
- **Primary:** "Your AI that does things — and proves it."
- **Secondary:** "Ask naturally. Watch it plan. Approve only real risk."
- **Technical:** "Governed AI execution with receipts."

### Writing style
- Use active voice
- Keep sentences short
- Skip corporate buzzwords
- Be specific, not vague
- Show, don't tell
